Professional Structural Engineering Solutions Across England & Wales

Safe, practical, and cost-effective structural solutions that comply with current UK Building Regulations and industry standards.

Whether you are planning a home extension, removing a load-bearing wall, constructing a new building, or undertaking major structural alterations, we provide detailed structural calculations, technical drawings, and engineering advice from concept to completion.

What We Offer

Our Structural Engineering Services

Structural Calculations

We prepare accurate structural calculations for residential and commercial projects, ensuring structural safety and Building Control compliance. Our calculations include steel beams, foundations, floor systems, roof structures, and load-bearing elements.

Image coming soon Structural Calculations

Steel Beam Design (RSJ Calculations)

Professional calculations for steel beams (RSJs) required for load-bearing wall removal, open-plan living spaces, house extensions, loft conversions, and garage conversions. All calculations are prepared to satisfy Building Control requirements.

Structural Reports

We provide structural reports for property purchases, structural defects, cracking investigations, insurance purposes, and building assessments — with practical recommendations and engineering solutions.

Foundation Design

Every building begins with a strong foundation. We provide foundation designs suitable for new build properties, house extensions, commercial developments, and retaining structures.

Retaining Wall Design

We design safe and efficient retaining walls capable of resisting earth pressure while complying with UK engineering standards — suitable for garden retaining walls, basement projects, and landscaping works.

Roof Structure Design

Our engineers design timber and steel roof structures for new build homes, loft conversions, roof alterations, and commercial buildings.
