What We Offer

Our Architectural Services

Planning Drawings

Professional planning drawings prepared to support planning permission applications for residential and commercial developments. Our drawings are designed to present your proposal clearly while meeting local authority planning requirements.

Building Regulations Drawings

Detailed technical drawings prepared in accordance with current UK Building Regulations. These drawings provide contractors and building control officers with the information required for construction approval.

House Extensions

Whether you're planning a rear extension, side extension, wraparound extension, or double-storey extension, we design spaces that maximise your property's potential while maintaining functionality and aesthetics.

Loft Conversions

Transform unused roof space into functional bedrooms, home offices, bathrooms, or living areas with professionally designed loft conversion plans.

Garage Conversions

Convert your existing garage into additional living accommodation, home offices, gyms, or guest rooms with practical architectural solutions.

New Build Design

From concept development through to construction drawings, we provide complete architectural design services for new residential and commercial developments.

Internal Alterations

We prepare layout redesigns and construction drawings for internal refurbishments, open-plan living spaces, and structural alterations.
