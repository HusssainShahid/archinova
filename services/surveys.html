What We Offer

Our Structural Survey & Inspection Services

Crack Investigation

Not all cracks indicate serious structural problems—but some require immediate professional assessment. Our structural engineers investigate cracks in external and internal walls, ceilings, floors, foundations, brickwork, and masonry. We determine the likely cause of movement and recommend the most appropriate structural solution.

Structural Inspection Reports

Professional assessment of your property's structural condition covering foundations, walls, floors, roof structures, chimneys, beams, lintels, and structural timber. Each report includes findings, recommendations, and where necessary proposed remedial solutions — suitable for homeowners, buyers, landlords, estate agents, solicitors, and insurance claims.

Pre-Purchase Structural Surveys

Our pre-purchase structural surveys help identify structural defects before you commit to buying. We assess structural movement, cracking, foundation condition, roof structure, damp-related structural damage, load-bearing elements, and signs of previous structural alterations.

Snagging Surveys

Our snagging surveys identify construction defects and workmanship issues before your developer's warranty period expires — covering structural defects, cracking, settlement, uneven floors, wall alignment, roof construction, and finishes. A detailed report helps ensure defects are rectified promptly by the developer.
