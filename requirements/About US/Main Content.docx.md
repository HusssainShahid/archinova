**Party Wall Awards**

We assist clients throughout the Party Wall Award process, ensuring all necessary documentation is professionally prepared and helping construction proceed in accordance with the agreed Award.

Our goal is to minimise delays while protecting the interests of all parties involved.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Do I always need a Party Wall Notice?**

Not always. Whether a Party Wall Notice is required depends on the type of work being carried out and its proximity to neighbouring properties.

---

**What is a Party Wall Award?**

A Party Wall Award is a legally binding document that sets out how certain construction works should proceed while protecting the interests of adjoining property owners.
