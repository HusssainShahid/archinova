**Snagging Surveys**

Purchasing a new-build home doesn't always mean it's free from defects.

Our snagging surveys identify construction defects and workmanship issues before your developer's warranty period expires.

Typical items inspected include:

* Structural defects

* Cracking

* Settlement

* Poor workmanship

* Uneven floors

* Wall alignment

* Roof construction

* External finishes

* Internal finishes

Our detailed snagging report helps ensure defects are rectified promptly by the developer.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**When should I arrange a structural survey?**

A structural survey is recommended when purchasing a property, investigating cracks, planning structural alterations, or if you have concerns about the condition of a building.
